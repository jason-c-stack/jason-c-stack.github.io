2026 Preprint

Posh Parking Spaces

Jason Stack and Nathan Williams

arXiv:2610.07280 [math.CO]

Classifies faithful, group-stable homogeneous systems of parameters for irreducible complex reflection groups by their degree and representation, and determines when the resulting quotient is a permutation module.

arXiv
2025 Preprint

Parking Spaces for Complex Reflection Groups

Jason Stack

arXiv:2502.01970 [math.CO]

Extends combinatorial and algebraic noncrossing parking spaces to irreducible well-generated complex reflection groups, proves equivariant isomorphisms in the stated cases, enumerates the resulting parking functions, and develops a Fuss generalization.

arXiv
2025 Article

An Elaborate New Proof of Cayley’s Formula

Esther Banaian, Anh Trong Nam Hoang, Elizabeth Kelley, Weston Miller, Jason Stack, Carolyn Stephen, and Nathan Williams

Algebraic Combinatorics 8 (4), 971–995

Constructs a bijection between selected Deodhar components of an affine braid variety and vertex-labeled trees, then combines it with affine Hecke-algebra tools to obtain an elaborate proof of Cayley’s formula.

Journal arXiv
2022 Article

Two Infinite Families of Elliptic Curves with Rank Greater Than One

Jeffrey Hatley and Jason Stack

INTEGERS 22, A1

Gives explicit infinite families of elliptic curves with uniformly controlled lower bounds on Mordell–Weil rank, beginning with elementary constructions and strengthening the results through additional arithmetic machinery.
